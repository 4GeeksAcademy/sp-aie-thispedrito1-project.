# HealthCore — The company

## Our history
HealthCore was founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas, by Dr. Sandra Okonkwo, a physician who spent years inside large hospital systems. She started HealthCore because clinicians were spending more time on paperwork than with patients. She is still our CEO and she is sceptical of any technology that does not solve a real problem.

## Our clinic network
HealthCore runs a network of 12 ambulatory clinics. Nine are in the United States (Texas, Florida and Georgia) and three are in the United Kingdom (London and Manchester). Each clinic has historically run its own processes, which is one of the reasons HealthCore Digital exists today.

## Service lines
Our clinics offer four service lines: primary care, specialist consultations, chronic disease management and preventive health programmes. We do not run hospitals or emergency departments.

## Our value proposition
Patients choose HealthCore for accessibility. That means three concrete promises: same-day appointments, extended opening hours, and bilingual care (English and Spanish) in our US markets. Every corporate team exists to protect those three promises.

## Size and regulation
HealthCore employs around 200 people and has an annual revenue of around 28 million US dollars. We operate under two health data protection frameworks: HIPAA in the United States and UK GDPR in the United Kingdom. They are different laws with different rules, and every system we build must respect the one that applies.

## Leadership and corporate teams
The CEO is Dr. Sandra Okonkwo. James Osei is the CTO and leads HealthCore Digital, the internal unit that builds systems, workflows and intelligent tools. Clinical Operations is led by Dr. Marcus Reid. Patient Experience and Access is led by Priya Nair from London. Revenue Cycle and Billing is led by Tom Callahan. Compliance and Data Governance is led by Claire Whitfield. People and Workforce is led by Diane Foster, VP of People.

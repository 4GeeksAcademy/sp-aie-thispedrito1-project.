# Compliance guidelines — UK GDPR (United Kingdom)

## Why this stage comes first
You will not receive access to any system that contains patient information until you pass this stage and Compliance approves it. There are no shortcuts: if you need more time, take it.

## What UK GDPR protects
UK GDPR protects personal data. Health data is special category data and needs extra protection: any information about a patient's physical or mental health, such as a diagnosis, an appointment at a clinic or an NHS number linked to a name.

## The minimum necessary principle
UK GDPR calls it data minimisation: process only the personal data that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purpose. Internally we use the same phrase as our US colleagues, minimum necessary. If a report works with counts, do not export patient-level rows. Having technical access to data does not mean you are allowed to look at it.

## Where health data may and may not go
Health data may only live in systems that Compliance has approved and that are covered by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with the vendor. It must never be pasted into Slack, Jira, Notion, personal email or any AI tool that is not approved.

## Access logging
Every access to a system with patient data is recorded in an access log, including who accessed which record and when. Compliance reviews these logs. Use your own account only: never share credentials.

## Incident notification
If you suspect that patient data was accessed, sent or exposed without authorisation, report it to Compliance the same day you notice it. Do not try to investigate on your own. Under UK GDPR, a personal data breach must be reported to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, when it is likely to put people at risk. Seventy-two hours includes weekends, so internal reporting cannot wait.

## Patients' rights
Patients can ask for a copy of their data (a subject access request). We must answer within one month. If you receive such a request, forward it to Compliance immediately.

## Who to ask
Compliance questions go to Claire Whitfield and her team. If the official material does not answer your question, ask Compliance rather than guessing.

# Your role — HealthCore Digital engineer

## Responsibilities
You build and maintain the systems, integrations and intelligent tools that clinical and operational teams use. You design for compliance from day one, write tests for what you build and document decisions so that others can maintain your work.

## Your first 90 days
In your first 90 days you contribute to the foundation phase: a minimum shared data model, standard network indicators, a reliable weekly executive dashboard and a pilot to reduce no-shows in one market. Your direct manager agrees your personal goals for these 90 days in your first week.

## Who you work with
You report to your direct manager in HealthCore Digital, part of James Osei's technology team. You work closely with Priya Nair's Patient Experience team on reminders, with Tom Callahan's Revenue Cycle team on data quality before billing, and with Claire Whitfield's Compliance team on any system that touches patient data.

## How you work with patient data
Engineers often have broad technical access. Use test or synthetic data whenever possible. Never copy production patient data to your laptop, a test environment or an AI tool. A new system or integration is approved through the process described in the "How we work" stage before it handles any patient data.

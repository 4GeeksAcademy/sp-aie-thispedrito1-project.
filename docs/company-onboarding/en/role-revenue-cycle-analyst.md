# Your role — Revenue Cycle analyst

## Responsibilities
You monitor claims and denials, find the root causes of denials and work with clinics to fix data quality before a claim is sent. You cover both revenue streams: US claims (commercial insurers, Medicare and Medicaid) and UK billing (private payment and the NHS contract).

## Your first 90 days
In your first 90 days you build a weekly denial report by cause, identify the three most frequent root causes of our 14% denial rate and propose one early validation that would stop a denial before the claim is sent. Your direct manager agrees your personal goals for these 90 days in your first week.

## Who you work with
You report to your direct manager in Revenue Cycle and Billing, led by Tom Callahan. You work with clinic front desks on booking data, with HealthCore Digital on reports and validations, and with Claire Whitfield's Compliance team whenever billing data leaves its system.

## How you work with patient data
Claims contain patient data. Use the minimum necessary for each analysis: aggregated reports by default and patient-level data only when the task requires it. Never export claims to personal spreadsheets or send them by email outside approved systems.

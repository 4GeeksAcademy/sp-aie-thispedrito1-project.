# How we work

## Our tools
We use four core tools. Jira tracks work and requests. Notion holds documentation, including the official policies. Slack is for day-to-day conversation. Microsoft 365 covers email, calendar and files. None of these four tools is approved for patient data.

## Working between Austin and London
London is usually six hours ahead of Austin. Our shared window is 9:00 to 11:00 in Austin, which is 15:00 to 17:00 in London: keep meetings that need both sites inside that window. Outside it, work asynchronously: write decisions down in Notion or in the Jira ticket so the other site can pick them up without a meeting.

## How a new system or integration is approved
No new system or integration may handle patient data until it is approved. The process has four steps: open a "System intake" request in Jira; HealthCore Digital does a technical review; Compliance reviews data protection, including a BAA for US vendors or a DPA for UK vendors; and the CTO gives the final approval. Skipping a step is not allowed, even for a pilot.

## Asking for help
Look in the official material in Notion first. If it does not answer your question, ask the owner of the topic: Compliance for compliance questions and People for everything else.

## In practice for HealthCore Digital {for: digital}
You will often be the person who opens the "System intake" request. Attach the data flow and the list of data fields to the request so Compliance can review it quickly.

## In practice for Revenue Cycle {for: revenue_cycle}
Billing tools and payer portals count as systems. If you want to connect a new payer portal or reporting tool, it goes through the same "System intake" process.

## In practice for other corporate teams {for: compliance, patient_experience, people, leadership}
Any tool you want to adopt, even a free one, is a new system. Open a "System intake" request before you sign up with company data.
